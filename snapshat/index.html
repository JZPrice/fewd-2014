About

What is this?

snapshat is a personal gut-health tracker. You snap a photo, match it to one of the seven types on the Bristol Stool Chart — a real clinical scale developed at the Bristol Royal Infirmary in 1997 — and the app builds a private record of your trends over time.

Why bother?

Stool form is one of the simplest, cheapest signals of digestive health. Patterns matter more than any single snap: chronic constipation (types 1–2), chronic loose stools (types 6–7), or sudden changes from your personal baseline are worth discussing with a clinician. Spotting changes early is the goal — not diagnosis.

Privacy

Everything lives in your browser (localStorage). Photos are never uploaded and never saved to disk — they only appear briefly during classification, then they're discarded. Only the type, date, notes, and flags are kept.

This is not medical advice

snapshat does not diagnose anything. If you notice blood, persistent pain, unexplained weight loss, or a sustained change in your bowel habits (especially over age 45), please talk to a clinician. Colorectal cancer is one of the most treatable cancers when caught early — routine screening (colonoscopy, FIT test, etc.) saves lives.
